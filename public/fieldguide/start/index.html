Get a starter key

A starter key is a scoped API key for the engines behind a blueprint. Getting one takes about a minute, it includes a $0.25 trial balance so your first calls cost nothing, and you top it up by card or USDC only when you're ready. No subscription, no crypto wallet required.

Your email is kept for support and service notices about this key, nothing else. Limit two keys per hour.

How billing works

Every engine call deducts its price from your key's balance. Starter keys are billed at the wholesale rate, currently half the public catalog price shown on the blueprint pages. When the balance runs out, calls simply stop; there is no overdraft and nothing recurring. Top up in $5, $25, or $100 packs whenever you like, by card on a Stripe payment page or by USDC on Base or Solana.

If anything goes wrong

Email info@theaslangroupllc.com with the subject "Starter key" and the blueprint name, and we'll provision one by hand, usually the same day.
