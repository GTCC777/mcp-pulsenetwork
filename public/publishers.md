# PulseNetwork Publishers & Media Kit

> Markdown mirror of https://pulse.theaslangroupllc.com/publishers — for AI agents and crawlers. Live counts: https://pulse.theaslangroupllc.com/.well-known/pulse-catalog.json

PulseNetwork is a media property operated by The Aslan Group LLC: a network of specialized digital publications covering travel, personal finance, health, climate, sports analysis, crypto safety, careers, real estate, insurance, and more. Each "endpoint" is a recurring column or briefing format within a property.

**What makes us unusual as a publisher is distribution.** Our content is requested and read by AI assistants on a person's behalf — inside Claude, inside autonomous shopping or research agents, or through direct API integration. The person still receives the recommendation; they receive it inside their assistant's answer rather than on a web page. Every property also has a human-readable home page at `<vertical>.theaslangroupllc.com`.

## How recommendations surface

Each property returns a researched, structured answer to a specific question (a destination weather brief, a deal roundup, a token safety scan). Where a merchant recommendation is genuinely relevant, we place the link ourselves, server-side, next to the content it supports. No ad network, no auction: editorial code we write and maintain decides where a link belongs. Merit-first: a commission never decides a pick, and affiliate links are always disclosed (see /affiliate-disclosure).
